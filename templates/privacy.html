Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

To provide our expense tracking service, we collect the information you choose to provide, including your name, email address, and any expense entries you log — such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

We also collect basic technical information automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, and device information, to help us maintain and improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect solely to operate, maintain, and improve Spendly. This includes generating the summaries and reports you request, authenticating your account, and responding to support inquiries.

We do not use your expense data to build advertising profiles and we do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers and protected by reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

You may access, update, or delete your expense entries and account information at any time. If you choose to close your account, we will delete your personal data in accordance with our data retention practices, except where retention is required by law.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of trusted third party providers to operate the service — for example, hosting infrastructure and transactional email delivery. These providers receive only the information necessary to perform their function and are contractually obligated to protect your data.

We do not share your expense data with advertisers, analytics brokers, or social media platforms. If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and notify active users before the change takes effect.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, our handling of your data, or would like to exercise your rights, please reach out to us through the contact information provided on our website. We will do our best to respond in a timely manner.
